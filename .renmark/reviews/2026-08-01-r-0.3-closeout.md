## Decision

**RECOMMEND: ACCEPTED, no follow-up.**

Unlike R-0.1 (2 follow-ups) and R-0.2 (materially larger set, 5+ follow-ups
from under-wired mechanisms), R-0.3's narrow scope — four schemas, one
writer, one reader, one real call site per kind — closed clean on the first
pass. The independent review (WP-6) found no gap, bug, or scope-creep risk
and confirmed every "carried, not re-opened" boundary held (F2/F5 fields
exist as schema shape only; no production caller constructs either
variant). This is a direct result of the Owner's explicit scope discipline
at kickoff ("do not let [F1/F2/F4/F5] expand R-0.3 unless they block the
ledger implementation") — none did.

## What R-0.3 does NOT close

Per the contract's explicit exclusions — these remain open for a future
release, not silently dropped:

- Blueprint schema, full milestone-contract schema, milestone-closeout
  schema (the rest of Phase 3's artifact list).
- Ledger snapshot/restore.
- Artifact content-hashing.
- Migration adapters for existing plan/review/memory artifacts into the
  ledger shape.
- Universal rewiring of every dispatch path to emit ledger events — only
  `execute_plan`'s real-run path emits Work Order/Result today; `dry_run`
  and any future dispatch route do not yet.
- R-0.2's F1/F2/F4/F5 themselves remain open (this milestone tracked them,
  did not close them — see contract's "Carried dependencies" section for
  the per-item disposition).
